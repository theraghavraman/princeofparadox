00 / The premise

A paradox is not merely a puzzle.
It is a pressure test for thought.

These thought experiments expose places where our intuitions, language, mathematics, morality or concepts of identity stop fitting neatly together.

Don't rush to solve them. Read the setup. Follow every implication. Notice where your intuition changes.
